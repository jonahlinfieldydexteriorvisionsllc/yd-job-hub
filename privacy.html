Privacy Policy

YD Job Hub

Last updated 30 September 2026

The short version. YD Job Hub is a private tool used by YD Exterior Visions LLC to run its own business. It is not open to the public, there is no advertising in it, and none of the information in it is sold or shared for marketing. The only people who can sign in are the owner and crew members the owner has approved.

Who this is

YD Job Hub is operated by YD Exterior Visions LLC, a landscaping, hardscaping and snow removal business near Madison, Wisconsin. Questions about this policy or about information held in the app should go to jonahlinfield@ydexteriorvisions.com.

Who uses the app

Two kinds of people sign in, both with a Google account:

  • The owner, who can see everything.
  • Crew members, approved individually by the owner. A crew member sees the work they need to do it — the property name and address on a snow route, and their own hours. They cannot see customer phone numbers, email addresses, pricing, job costs, or anybody else's hours. This is enforced by the database's own access rules, not merely by what the screens choose to show.

Customers of YD Exterior Visions do not have accounts and cannot sign in.

What the app holds

AboutWhat
Customers and prospective customers Name, property address, phone number, email address, notes about the work, quoted and invoiced amounts
Jobs and snow work Service details, materials, site notes, property measurements, snow route order, arrival and departure times, snow depth cleared, salt used
People who sign in Name and email address from their Google account, the role the owner assigned, and for crew their hourly rate
Crew hours Clock-in and clock-out times, what they were working on, pauses and the reason given, and the wages those hours come to
Faults When something goes wrong, the error message, which screen it happened on, the device type, and a short trail of the last few actions. Text typed into forms is deliberately not recorded.

No payment card details, bank details or government identification numbers are collected or stored. The app does not track location in the background; a crew member's device location is used only at the moment they tap to record arriving at or leaving a property.

Where it is kept, and who else sees it

Information is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) on servers in the United States, and a copy is held on the device of whoever is signed in so the app keeps working with no signal. Sign-in is handled by Google Authentication; the app never sees or stores anybody's Google password.

Nothing is sold, rented, or handed to advertisers. Information is disclosed outside the business only where the law requires it.

The QuickBooks connection

If the owner connects the app to QuickBooks Online, the connection is authorised through Intuit's own sign-in, and the app never sees the QuickBooks password. The permission granted is used only to read the customer list and the current invoice numbering, and to create invoices for work recorded in the app. The connection can be cut at any time, from the app or from within QuickBooks, and cutting it stops all further access immediately.

How long it is kept

Job, invoice and crew-hour records are kept for as long as the business needs them and as long as tax and employment record-keeping requires. Fault reports are kept only until the fault has been dealt with. A person who signs in and is not approved is recorded as having asked, and nothing more.

Asking about your information

A customer, prospective customer or crew member may ask what the business holds about them, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to jonahlinfield@ydexteriorvisions.com. Records the business is legally required to keep — invoices and payroll among them — cannot be deleted on request, and the reason will be given.

Children

The app is a tool for running a business. It is not directed at children and no part of it is intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If what the app does with information changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
